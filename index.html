Blackjack Card-Counting Simulator

Probabilistic modeling & rigorous statistical investigation of player expected value (EV%).

The Challenge

Investigated how accurately the Hi-Lo card counting system predicts player advantage (EV%) across True Count (TC) levels (-2 to +6) in a 6-deck shoe when 1 deck remains.

Methodology & Rigor

Programmed a Monte Carlo simulation running 180,000 total rounds (20,000 rounds per TC level). Solved edge-case bugs in deck normalization, dealer S17 rules, and multi-split hand logic.

Statistical Results

Achieved R² = 0.996 (Pearson r = 0.998). Calculated Standard Errors (SE = 0.675%), 95% Confidence Intervals (±1.35%), Z-values (<0.36), and physics-based slope bounds (0.5525 vs 0.543 theoretical).
